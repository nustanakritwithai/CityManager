# Residential density

Residential buildings keep their original ID and one-cell footprint as they grow automatically. Existing cottage levels 1–3 retain their capacities of 1/4, 2/8 and 3/12 households/persons. The following are **provisional game-balance values**, to be calibrated after performance diagnosis and economic playtesting:

| Tier | Level | Households | Persons |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Apartment | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| Apartment | 2 | 8 | 32 |
| Apartment | 3 | 12 | 48 |
| Condominium | 1 | 16 | 64 |
| Condominium | 2 | 24 | 96 |
| Condominium | 3 | 32 | 128 |

Growth keeps the original monthly checks and 200-credit outside construction investment. It adds vacancies, never people. Households, wallets, employment and claims remain intact. Admission still requires the existing jobs, affordability, road and utility checks; the city-wide 128-household safeguard remains in place. Utilities and rent are charged per actual household under existing rules. A capacity larger than available city-wide room is not a guarantee that it can be filled.

Higher tiers use bounded procedural silhouettes within the same cell, with no external models. Save v16/chain-11 accepts the existing park activity and recreation session as one atomic envelope; v15 and older are checked under their original housing limits before conversion. A bounded backup of the previous v15 bytes is made before replacing the primary save. The integration owner must reconcile this provisional version with other progression work before release.
